04 · How I decide

Four rules I actually use.

  1. 01

    Configuration over customisation. say no to client-specific builds so the platform stays reliable.

  2. 02

    Safety before adoption speed. gate AI output until it's accurate enough for production banking traffic.

  3. 03

    Earned simplicity. map every edge case, then deliberately decide what not to build.

  4. 04

    Own it. Ship it. Defend it.
